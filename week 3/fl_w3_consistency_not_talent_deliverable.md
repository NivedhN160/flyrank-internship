## 🎯 1. The Through-Line (One-Line Claim & Content Map)

### One-Line Claim:
> **"I build resilient, production-ready software from bare-metal C kernels to agentic FastAPI backends—and here is the verifiable code to prove it."**

### Content Map & CTA Hierarchy:
* **Section 1: Hero Header** → Name, Role, Education (GPA 9.55+), Claim.

* **Section 2: Lead Systems Case Study (Strongest Work First)** → **N-OS (Bare-Metal 32-bit OS in C/Assembly)** with custom TCP/IP stack and VFS.  
  * *CTA 2:* `[View N-OS Kernel Source & Architecture]`.
* **Section 3: AI & Transformer Deep-Dive** → **ZigNGPT v2 (Transformer from Scratch in Zig)** with matrix-level self-attention.  
  * *CTA 3:* `[Inspect Zig Matrix Engine & Commits]`.
* **Section 4: Cloud & Backend Architecture** → **Docker Postgres Storage Repository**, **LLM Triage API**, and **FlyRank Capstone Backends**.  
  * *CTA 4:* `[View Docker Architecture]` & `[View Backend Capstones]`.
* **Section 5: Personal Philosophy & Identity** → Bio, values, and standing style note.
* **Section 6: Footer & Contact** → Standing links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and Email.

## 🎨 2. Personal Identity Kit (Frame, Not Upstage)

* **Design Rule:** The design is the frame, not the painting. The work is the painting.
* **Typography:**
  * Headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Bold 700 / SemiBold 600)
  * Body: `Inter` (Regular 400 / Medium 500)
  * Monospace / Code: `JetBrains Mono`
* **Color Palette:**
  * Background: `#F8FAFC` (Slate Light — clean, calm negative space)
  * Surface Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  * Text: `#0F172A` (Slate Dark — high legibility)
  * Accent: `#2563EB` (Royal Blue — focused single accent)
  * Muted / Borders: `#64748B` / `#E2E8F0`
* **Monogram Logo:** Minimalist vector SVG monogram (`logo.svg`).
* **Standing Style Note:**
  > Headings in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, body in `Inter`. Palette: `#F8FAFC` (bg), `#0F172A` (text), `#2563EB` (accent), `#64748B` (muted). Clean, distraction-free, letting verifiable technical repositories stand out.

## 🖼️ 3. Image Curation & Discernment (Real Captures > AI Mockups)

* **The Core Choice:** Real captures of working systems build immediate recruiter credibility; AI-generated fake code illustrations erode trust.
* **Curated Asset Inventory:**
  1. *Real Bio Photo* (authentic human identity)
  2. *QEMU Terminal Boot Screen* (N-OS kernel execution)
  3. *FastAPI Swagger UI* (interactive `/docs` schema)
  4. *Docker Compose Runtime Log* (PostgreSQL 16 + Redis multi-container orchestration)
  5. *Pytest Green Pass Log* (6/6 automated test proofs)
  6. *Subtle Vector Geometric Banner* (`hero_texture.svg`)
* **Ruthless Rejections Documented:**
  * ❌ *Rejected:* Glowing 3D cyberpunk server renders (distracting, zero proof value).
  * ❌ *Rejected:* AI synthetic developer avatar (artificial, creates disconnect).
  * ❌ *Rejected:* Abstract AI neural network mesh (visual noise that upstages content).
